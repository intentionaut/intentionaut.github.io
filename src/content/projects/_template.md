## The Problem

Two or three short sentences. Who hurts, and why the existing options fail.
Concrete images beat abstractions. No marketing voice.

## The Bet

What this product believes that its competitors don't. Name the bet in one
sentence, then show how the product acts on it. This section is about the
idea, not the feature list.

## Under the Hood

- **Bold lead:** detail in plain words, one clause of colour at most
- **Another lead:** keep each bullet scannable
- Facts only here: architecture, data, constraints, how it was built

## Outcomes

Numbers when you have them: users, revenue, retention, adoption, time saved.
Even one honest figure beats none. If there are no numbers yet, say why
(beta stage) and what you expect to measure. A hiring product leader reads
this section first.

## Why it Matters

Three or four sentences. Who is using it, and what changes for them.
Close on the philosophy, not on a sales line.
Let the reader infer ambition from scope; never say "I did everything".
