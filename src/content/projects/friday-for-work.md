## The Problem

Breaking into film and TV runs on word of mouth and closed networks. Early-career crew have empty credits and a last job behind a bar. Generic AI tools make it worse: they hand you a polished CV you can't defend when someone reads it back to you.

## The Bet

Friday bets that coaching beats writing. It scores your CV against how this industry actually hires: how credits get read, why crew-list order matters, what a first job in the camera department is really asking for. Then it asks questions until you can tell your own story. That happens to be the exact skill the room tests next.

## Under the Hood

- **Domain data:** patterns of how productions actually hire are encoded into the product, so advice stops averaging every industry at once
- **It remembers:** answers follow you into every future cover letter; practice interviews know which role you're chasing
- **Outcomes feed back:** logged interviews, offers, rejections and silences shape what Friday suggests next
- Designed, built and shipped in one week with AI-assisted development from start to finish

## Outcomes

Users in private beta are reporting more call-backs, interviews and hires. As the product develops I'll write about Friday here, and update this section with more data.

## Why it Matters

Friday is in private beta with crew using it to land their first roles. They walk into rooms able to defend every line on the page. Self-representation is a skill, not a document. And it's the skill that decides who gets a chance.
