## The Problem

A Mac menu bar fills up. Every app that runs in the background adds an icon, and on a laptop with a notch the ones on the left end up behind it, out of reach. Good tools for this already exist. I wanted to know whether I could build something small, useful and local myself.

## The Bet

Snug hides the icons you don't need to see. Click the mousehole in the menu bar and a dropdown shows three rows: Showing, Hidden and Always hidden. Click an icon to use it. Drag it to another row to move it.

Snug asks for two permissions and explains each one before macOS does. Nothing it sees leaves your Mac. Icons beside the notch move too, which was the hardest part to build.
