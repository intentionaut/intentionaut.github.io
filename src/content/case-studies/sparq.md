## The problem

- Fewer than half of customers find traditional paid advertising credible.
- Dealers were late adopters of digital, with little ability to work across channels.
- Shoppers often vented about buying a car on social media, but rarely engaged with the dealership.

Dealers also struggled to use the data their digital properties produced, and the cost of doing online business as usual kept rising.

## How it works

1. The salesperson takes a photo at the moment of purchase.
2. The photo is posted to the dealership's feed and the customer's, with a testimonial or review.
3. The more friends who "SPARQ" the photo, the better the prize the customer wins.
4. Friends who click the photo get email and display campaigns, carrying the word-of-mouth effect.

## My role

I worked on SPARQ from concept to launch, in a team led by our Art Director. As UX Principal, I led the UX research, built it into the design, and supported the development team. I also built and led the team that designed and delivered SPARQ's managed services and service extensions.

The objectives were set early. The product had to stand apart from competitors in an obvious way. It had to appeal to shoppers and to dealers. It had to be a base other services could be built on. And it had to be tested for market fit and usability before those services launched.

## What the research found

People wanted to show off their new car. So SPARQ had to be there at that moment, and help dealers capture it: post the sale, then retarget the buyer's friends and family across their Facebook networks.

Buyers also didn't want to wait around to have their picture taken. So we made the photo, and the reward, easy to take and easy to claim. The reward for having the photo taken included free oil changes and other maintenance, which brought buyers back to the dealership and gave dealers new chances to keep them as customers.

## What it produced

SPARQ launched in late 2014 and gave dealers their own channel for word-of-mouth marketing with customers and their friends.

- 140+ clients signed in under 120 days.
- 60% of customers claimed their photos.
- 14% of claimed photos led to a referral sale.
- Average email open rate of 32%, more than ten times the e-commerce average, and an average click-through rate of 27%.
- Page followers up to 85 times higher in 90 days.
- Social referral traffic up 35 times on average.

## Why it matters

The best advertising for a car dealer was already happening, in the buyer's own feed. SPARQ's design job was to make that moment easy to capture and worth sharing, and then to keep it working.
