## The problem

Delivery was the weak point of a model that depended on it. More orders were missing their delivery date. The Trustpilot rating fell 0.2, under the 4.5 that counts as excellent, and continued to around 4.2.

A car that sits costs money, because it depreciates. So the aim was to turn stock faster: to increase the average vehicle turnover rate, as part of the logistics tooling.

## The case

There were two objections. "We have a vendor for this", from the Director of Operations, who oversaw the vendor relationship. And "product management can do this with engineering". The CEO was aligned with the thinking, so we planned the case together.

I built the case for internalising logistics against the product team's position, and drafted the commercial strategy that went to the CEO for adding product and design to the internalised operations division. First I drove the event storming across the logistics and returns flow, to establish what building logistics end to end would take and what infrastructure it needed. It gave a clear view of that, and stakeholder alignment on it.

The price was explicit. Standing up the division meant delaying customer features in the storefront, so teams could run discovery with the operation and take it over.

## What it produced

Average days to sale fell from 34 to 29 in 2019, against an Auto Trader market average of 34 days in May 2019. In 2020 we drove it to 21 days on average; the market was at 23 that September. In 2021 we held an average of 24 days, in a year when the market started at 43 and reached 23 by July. Cost per sale fell by more than £900, a 45% reduction per vehicle. The Trustpilot rating recovered to 4.5 after the operations division took on delivery and support.

The division's users were Cazoo's own drivers and technicians, and I owned CSAT on first delivery and time to first delivery as their metrics. Its work included tracking, which moved from manual toward automation, and what the business measured: throughput, over other measures of efficiency.
