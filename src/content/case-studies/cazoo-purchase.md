## The problem

Cazoo was a low-margin, high-volume business, and in a competitive market it could not grow without more cars. The cheapest cars to find were the ones people already owned, so Cazoo had to become a buyer as well as a seller.

The risk was expectations. Sellers were used to one move: a confident valuation online, then a haggle down at the moment of handover, on their own driveway. A buying business that sellers did not trust would not get the cars, and without the cars there was nothing to sell.

The work had three objectives:

- Give sellers a better experience than selling to the incumbents.
- Track every bought car efficiently, so it could go back to market fast. That work has its own page: [Cazoo logistics](/case-studies/cazoo-internal-tooling/).
- Add margin and revenue through a new way of acquiring vehicles.

## Listening to sellers

The team spoke to 15 people who had recently sold their cars, about what had gone well and what had not. We knew the primary emotional need was reassurance, so the selling journey had to give sellers the same reassurance we had created for buyers. The research showed that trust and consistency of the quote came first: sellers' biggest fear was being haggled down.

That pointed at the haggle, so the product was built as a single valuation: the price you see is the price you're paid when we pick up your car. We worked closely with valuations, data, commercial operations and sales to decide which promises customers could trust, and I shaped the roadmap around them.

Cazoo paid the agreed price in over 97% of cases. The exception was a car substantially more damaged than the seller had reported, and Cazoo held high thresholds on the cars it bought.

## What it produced

Keeping the promise created a compound advantage at the time: Cazoo bought a car from a customer, then sold them their next one, with high trust. Sellers came back: the average repeat purchase rate was 22%. The most repeat sales to a single customer was four. That family upgraded all of their cars with Cazoo and sold Cazoo three.

- Cazoo Purchase added margin and inventory.
- In Q3 2021, 10% of retail units sold had been sourced directly from consumers. The 38% above counts something else: Purchase's share of all transactions.
- User research went on to inform strategic acquisitions at Cazoo.
